# Candidates

Three candidate error lines drafted for "Say What Actually Broke":

1. Save failed: you had unsaved changes, we had a 3 a.m. auto-deploy, and only one of us was awake — it wasn't you. Your draft is gone; so is our dignity.
2. **Upload failed: the file was fine, you were fine — our server ran out of disk because someone's been storing cat videos in /tmp since 2021. Try again in 10 minutes.** ← picked (163 characters)
3. Payment failed: your card is fine, our checkout just panicked, charged its own test card $0.00 twelve times, and needs a minute with a paper bag to breathe into.

Picked #2: it names the real failure in plain words (the server ran out of disk), tells the user what to do next (try again in 10 minutes), lands its joke in a single read with a repeatable image (cat videos in /tmp since 2021), and would survive being pasted into a real product's error dialog.
